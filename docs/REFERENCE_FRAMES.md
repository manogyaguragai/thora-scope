## Optics
- **Mask / crop:** no circular mask is visible. The scope image is a hard-edged rectangle (≈1220×976 inside the screenshot, aspect 1.25 = 5:4), i.e. the image circle is cropped inside the sensor. The §8 "circular mask" therefore lies outside the visible area — use a 5:4 soft rounded-rect crop, or a circle radius ≥ half-diagonal that only darkens corners (frame wins per §8).
- **Vignette:** mean luma centre ≈152 vs corners ≈95–114 (≈25–35 % falloff), asymmetric and scene-driven; mostly headlight inverse-square falloff, not lens vignette. Use a mild lens vignette (≤0.2) and let the headlight do the rest.
- **Distortion:** mild-moderate barrel; rib lines bow strongly near edges (0:39, 06:29). k1 ≈ 0.12–0.18.
- **Grade / white balance:** strong red-magenta cast (mean RGB ≈ 184/117/125), high saturation (mean HSV S ≈ 0.39), ~13 % of pixels clip red (≥250). Near-field highlights blow out to white-pink; shadows go maroon, not grey. Steel reads neutral-to-warm.
- **Speculars:** sharp, small, white wet glints on lung, vein and fat that move with the scope; steel has long line highlights.
- **Noise / sharpness:** very little visible grain (high-pass residual ≈0.7 levels); image is soft, compression-smoothed with mild blocking. Use a near-zero grain plus slight softness rather than sensor noise. Chromatic aberration not discernible beyond compression fringing.
- **Resolution:** screenshots 1920×1080, but the content looks upscaled from SD (5:4 aspect consistent with 720×576 PAL source).
- **Lens fog / water:** 06:46 shows the underwater stump test: low contrast, orange-pink haze, blur.
